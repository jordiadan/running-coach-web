# Design System: Running Coach

## Overview

Baseline capture of the code at commit `46c8658`, with a scoped authenticated
portal update from `portal.css`, `weekly-plan.css`, `PortalPage.tsx`, Connect and
Profile on 2026-10-16. Working conventions live in AGENTS.md and product context
in PRODUCT.md. No canonical Pencil frame is tracked; this record does not claim
a comparison with Pencil. Observable sources are `src/index.css`,
`tailwind.config.ts`, `src/components/ui/` and their consuming screens.

The shared foundation combines readable sans-serif text and themed tonal
surfaces. Portal and onboarding retain serif headings and rounded controls.
Landing and Login use condensed athletic display type, square photography and
actions; the portal uses lists, metrics and session cards. A screen's composition
is not promoted to a global rule.

Frontmatter tokens record existing values, with warm as the base theme and
prefixes for the other declared variants. They index the source rather than
authorize changes. Check differences against the code and project instructions
before updating this record.

The authenticated extension carries Weekly Plan's existing type, theme roles and
geometry into Connect, Profile and their shared shell. Its brief is
`docs/portal-design-iteration.md`; it introduces no public-page direction or
rebrand. Global tokens and unrelated baseline observations remain as recorded;
this update does not claim to reconcile all pre-existing documentation drift.

The onboarding extension follows `docs/onboarding-design-iteration.md`, using
the same portal roles, typography and shared Connect/Profile controls. Its
compact setup path, source selection and disclosed setup facts are scoped to
first-run tasks; they introduce no new theme, tokens, dependencies or raster assets.

The public extension on 2026-10-16 is scoped to `/`, `/login`,
`src/components/public/public.css` and `PublicLayout.tsx`. Its direction contract
is `.impeccable/surfaces/src-pages-landingpage-tsx.md`. The implemented public
vocabulary is a photographic running publication: real athlete imagery,
condensed two-line headlines, an explorable example week and a broad action-color
close. It shares the portal's body font, track mark and four palettes, while its
public-only display font and square geometry remain separate from portal,
onboarding and privacy. Concept studies are not approved pixel specifications
or implementation evidence. No global construction preference or preferred
theme is established.

## Colors

### Primary

The base theme uses muted green `primary` for actions and emphasis, with
`primary-foreground` for content over that background. `ring` follows the
primary color for focus. Frontmatter preserves the source HSL values.

### Secondary

`secondary` and `secondary-foreground` serve secondary controls and backgrounds.
`muted` and `muted-foreground` distinguish supporting content. `accent` is the
warm accent used in details such as key-session indicators; no fixed color
distribution is established.

### Neutral

`background` and `foreground` define the document; `card` and `popover` separate
surfaces. `border`, `input` and `divider` have distinct roles even where their
values coincide. `surface-elevated` and `text-subtle` have their own utilities.
`destructive` and its foreground represent errors and destructive actions.

`src/index.css` declares Warm & Earthy, Dark & Bold, Clean & Bright and Forest &
Deep. `useTheme.tsx` defaults to warm and stores the selection in `rc-theme`.
The frontmatter's `dark-`, `bright-` and `forest-` prefixes describe those
variants, not independent colors to mix on a screen. The initial setup's computed
style capture confirmed the base theme. Its compiled CSS contained `.theme-warm`
but not the other three selectors. The portal iteration fixes that compatibility
defect with literal theme class names in `useTheme.tsx`, allowing Tailwind to
retain all four declared themes without changing their palettes. The historical
capture does not establish current theme rendering or contrast compliance.

The portal inherits foreground, background, surface-elevated, secondary, border,
primary and primary-foreground roles. Supporting text uses foreground at 70%
opacity; field borders use foreground at 45%. Its action pair follows the theme:
the warm theme supplies the forest-green active-source surface, rather than a
new fixed palette. Scoped contrast adjustments darken the dark theme's action
fill with 40% black. Bright mixes its primary text/focus role with 15% black and
its action fill with 30% black. Error text mixes destructive with 10% black on
light themes; dark and forest use `hsl(0 85% 75%)`. These overrides live in
`portal.css` and do not replace the global theme tokens. Weekly Plan consumes
these same primary, action and error roles, keeping
colored surfaces separate from primary text and focus. Their presence alone is
not a contrast-compliance verification.

Public pages inherit the same theme ground, foreground, secondary and action
roles. Supporting copy uses public-muted. The dark and bright action adjustments
match the portal's 40% and 30% black mixes. Public focus outlines use currentColor,
so controls on action fills retain their contrasting foreground. The action
field carries primary-foreground; its inverse action reverses that pair.
The desktop hero uses white copy over a monochrome photograph and a neutral black
scrim increasing from 5% over the athletes to 56% behind the copy. At 760px and
below, copy returns to the theme foreground on its ground
and the scrim is removed. Public error roles follow
the light/dark division recorded in frontmatter. These are local roles in
`public.css`, not replacements for the global palette.

## Typography

DM Serif Display with Georgia/serif remains the portal/onboarding heading and
brand family. DM Sans with system-ui/sans-serif remains shared body text and
controls. These families load from Google Fonts in `src/index.css`.

Public Landing and Login use RC Display, the self-hosted Barlow Condensed
semibold (600), for display headings and their brand wordmark. The font is
`src/assets/public/fonts/barlow-condensed-semibold.ttf`, declared by the scoped
public stylesheet with font-display swap and Arial Narrow/sans-serif fallbacks.
Its OFL license is stored beside the font. This public display addition is
intentional; it does not replace the portal or privacy type system.

Frontmatter captures the implemented public ramp. The landing display uses
public-display above 1050px, public-display-compact at 1050px and below, and
public-display-mobile at 760px and below. Public plan/story headings use
public-section-heading, changing to `clamp(2.75rem, 10vw, 4rem)` at 760px and
below. Login's single task heading uses public-login-heading, changing to
`clamp(3rem, 10vw, 4rem)` at 760px and below. The closing heading uses
`clamp(2.75rem, 5vw, 4.5rem)` with line-height 1, then the same mobile section
size. Utility text, day/session labels and the New here heading use DM Sans.
Public introductory copy is 16–18px; metadata and supporting notes are 13–14px.

Weekly Plan, Connect, Profile and onboarding task headings use the portal-heading
role, switching to portal-heading-wide from 640px (40px / 48px). Connect and Profile
supporting copy commonly uses 13–15px; editable fields use 16px. Portal loading
and authentication states retain their local heading sizes. Onboarding uses the
same 40px / 48px task headings, 18px / 20px source labels, 20px section headings,
and 13–15px supporting text. Its heading focus outline
retains the incumbent 4px corner radius. These scoped values supplement the
abbreviated frontmatter ramp.
Session metrics, including the public example week, use tabular numbers.
There is no global body-text width limit in ch; do not impose one by default.

## Layout

CSS. Privacy retains `max-w-2xl` (42rem) and its existing layout. Public Landing
and Login share a 1440px maximum including fluid public-gutter padding. Their
normal-flow masthead is 88px tall, 76px at 760px and below. The hero has its own
inset width rather than the standard content gutter. Public sections use
fluid vertical spacing; portal spacing retains the existing Tailwind rhythm.

Default breakpoints are sm (640px), md (768px), lg (1024px), xl (1280px) and
2xl (1536px). Tailwind's `container` config sets a 1400px width at 2xl; that is
not the breakpoint. Profile essentials use two columns from sm. Template
components in `src/components/ui/` do not establish a global sidebar or a
multicolumn dashboard.

The authenticated header and standard portal content share a 72rem maximum,
with 24px horizontal padding. Onboarding uses a 47rem maximum, including that
same horizontal padding. Below 900px, the single set of
three destination links forms a fixed bottom bar with safe-area padding; from
900px it sits in the sticky header. Main content leaves room for the bottom bar.
The header keeps theme and logout controls available at both sizes.

Onboarding has no destination bar. A compact horizontal three-step path stays
above the task at every width. Source choices are stacked native radio rows with
one shared authorization action; capability differences remain visible. Profile
fields sit directly on the page, with essentials in two columns from 640px.
Onboarding weekday controls use seven columns from 380px and four below, with
44px minimum targets. Race-target choices occupy one row. Plan preparation and

Connect stacks provider surfaces on narrow screens, places them side by side
from 640px, and adds a 16rem guidance column from 900px. Profile is one column
below 900px, with compact identity above the editor; from 900px the editor leads
beside a 17.5rem identity/zones aside. Profile running-day controls use four

then seven columns with 80px minimum height. Weekly Plan's date navigator uses
four columns below 400px to retain 44px-wide targets, then seven columns. Weekly
Plan retains its 1024px workspace breakpoint.

Public Landing places a training-group photograph behind right-hand title,
copy and action. The hero is inset 24px on each side, capped at 1440px, with

The illustrative plan sits beside a companion photograph. Its weekday strip
has seven columns above 1050px and four at 1050px and below; session names stay
visible at every width. Editorial session content uses two columns, with notes
moving below at 1050px. At 760px the plan and photo stack, with a 240px photo.

## Elevation & Depth

The shared implementation uses tonal surfaces, borders and Tailwind shadows.
Base cards use `shadow-sm`; raised or open controls can use larger shadows.
Screens reference `shadow-card`, but the inspected CSS and config define no
custom token for it; do not invent a value. Today cards and their states are
observed behavior rather than a universal recipe.
Public Landing and Login use flat grounds, an action-color closing field and
square image clipping without raised cards or blurred navigation. The desktop
hero's neutral scrim supports white copy; the mobile photo has no scrim.

CSS animates body color changes over 0.3s. The portal uses opacity and translation
transitions, springs and expansions; some screens consult `useReducedMotion`.
Public photographs reveal with a short clip shutter: inset-left 12% resolves
to 0 in 850ms using the public easing curve. Images start visible; useInView
triggers this once at 18% visibility, including below-fold photographs. Hero
title lines rise through individual masks from 105% over 750ms, delayed 120ms
and 210ms. Supporting copy/actions resolve from 0.4 opacity over 450ms; Login
uses a 160ms delay. Selected weekday fill uses a shared-layout spring (stiffness
420, damping 38), and session detail enters with 6px travel and opacity over
220ms. Actions use 180ms feedback, a 0.985 press scale and 4px horizontal arrow
travel. The pending spinner runs at 1s while waiting.

Public links progressively enhance same-document route changes with native view
transitions: photo 450ms with public easing, brand 300ms and root 200ms. Route
updates are flushed before the destination image decode and snapshot completes.
Modified clicks, unsupported browsers and reduced motion retain ordinary links.
MotionConfig, useReducedMotion and scoped CSS show final states and remove
shutter/title travel, layout animation, transitions and spinner animation under
reduced motion. This record does not claim application-wide reduced-motion
coverage or accessibility certification.

Portal provider/editor surfaces use a restrained shadow
(`0 8px 24px -16px hsl(var(--foreground) / 0.22)`). Settings controls and navigation
use 160ms state transitions; shell destination changes use 160ms opacity and 4px
translation. Scoped reduced-motion rules remove portal animations, transitions
and active-state translation, and the shell consults `useReducedMotion`.

## Shapes

The base radius variable is 0.625rem; `lg` uses it and `md`/`sm` subtract 2px/4px.
Bright declares 0.75rem. Portal cards also use Tailwind's `rounded-xl` and
`rounded-2xl`; chips and indicators use circular shapes. These classes do not
all depend on the radius variable. Preserve the distinction between semantic
radii and component-specific radii.

The portal extension repeats 12px control and 16px surface radii, following
Weekly Plan. Profile day controls use 10px corners; identity monograms and small
selection indicators remain circular. These scoped values do not change the
base UI library's radius variable.

Public photographs, actions, weekday selections and public theme controls use
square corners. The provider mark's circular white backing remains a local
exception. Public square geometry does not change portal surface radii or
shared UI primitives.

## Components

### Buttons

`src/components/ui/button.tsx` defines default, destructive, outline, secondary,
ghost, link, hero and hero-outline. Base height is 40px; sm is 36px; lg is 44px.
Hero retains the primary color and uses base text with wide tracking.
Outline/ghost retain their existing states.

Focus uses a 2px ring with a 2px offset. Disabled reduces opacity and blocks
interaction. `asChild` composes the consumer's element; verify semantics when
reusing it. Some CTAs have local size overrides.

Portal account controls retain 44px targets. Provider actions are at least 46px
high; Profile save and editable-field controls are at least 48px high. Portal
focus uses a 2px primary outline with a 4px offset. These local sizes supplement
the base Button variants rather than redefining every button as 40px.

Public actions use public-action tokens with 56px minimum height and square
corners. The Google action fills its form region and includes the provider mark;
the closing action reverses the theme-adjusted action pair. Hover moves the
trailing arrow; pressed state scales the action. Disabled session-check/OAuth
states retain a visible label and status and prevent duplicate requests. Public
keyboard focus uses a 2px currentColor outline with 5px offset.

### Cards / Containers

The base Card has lg radius, a border, card background, card-foreground text and
shadow-sm; internal slots generally use 24px padding. Portal-specific cards
vary with 16px/20px/24px padding and xl/2xl radii. Documentation preserves them.

### Inputs / Fields

The base Input uses input border, background fill, 40px height, 12px/8px padding
and md radius. Base text switches to sm from md; focus has a 2px ring and offset.
Profile composes existing labels, selects and calendar/popover controls. Errors
use destructive text and operations can disable controls.

The Profile editor overrides base inputs, selects, comboboxes and date controls
to the portal-input geometry, with ground fill and the scoped control border.
Read-only fields use the soft surface; pending saves disable the editing
fieldset. Selection, validation and calculated zones remain distinct states.

### Navigation

Public Landing and Login share a condensed display wordmark, track mark,
normal-flow masthead, theme selector, contextual link and skip link. Landing
hides its story anchor at 760px and below; Login hides its home text at 420px
and below while retaining a labelled 44px control. The shared footer links to
privacy and the three photographic source pages. Brand/home/login links use the
progressive native transition described above.

The portal retains its serif brand, sticky header and Weekly Plan, Connect and
Profile destination links, placed as described in Layout. Links retain native
URL and history behavior through the `view` query parameter. The current
destination has an underline, stronger weight and tonal fill as well as color.
A skip link targets main content. The theme selector is a Radix dropdown. Its
availability does not establish a new navigation pattern for other pages.

### Public example week and imagery

Native weekday buttons expose pressed state and control one polite, atomic
session-detail region. A continuous action-colored selection links the day to
its prescription. Every width keeps both weekday and session name visible.
The selected session heading leads day metadata, duration, effort and notes.
The example is explicitly illustrative, not an editable or generated personal
plan. Login keeps one task heading and one Google action with checking, ready,
pending and error states, visible status and recoverable errors. Its unboxed
first-run guidance and Connect / Your profile / Your week path explain what
follows; authenticated sessions redirect to the portal.

Three real Unsplash photographs support distinct training contexts: Fitsum
Admasu's road-training group for the monochrome hero, Jakob Owens's runner beside
the water for the example plan, and Filip Mroz's track stride for setup and
Login. Source-page links appear in the footer; asset derivation and license
records live in `src/assets/public/README.md`. The images are atmosphere, not
customer endorsements. The training photograph has 900w/1800w WebP variants;
stride and runner use 1000px and 900px assets. Cover crops respond to their
specific contexts. No design-study image is presented as implementation evidence.

### Connect

The active provider is first and uses the theme's action surface and contrasting
text. Each provider keeps identity, status and capability before its explicit
Connect, Reconnect, Replace source or Disconnect action. The inactive source is
quiet. Disconnect and replacement use confirmation dialogs. Loading, unavailable
status/retry, pending authorization and operation failures retain visible feedback.
The provider composition is scoped to this screen, not a generic card template.

Portal confirmation dialogs carry the same scoped roles outside the shell's DOM
subtree. Their surfaces use 16px corners, buttons use 12px corners and 48px minimum
height, and confirmation buttons use the theme-adjusted action pair. Supporting
and destructive text use the portal's corresponding roles. The dialogs preserve
the portal focus outline and suppress dialog/overlay animation and transitions
under reduced motion.

### Profile

One editor groups essentials, weekly rhythm and race goal, with identity and
calculated zones as supporting context. Running-day buttons expose pressed state;
the four-day minimum and long-run select remain visible constraints. The save bar
becomes sticky only while dirty, saving or failed; its resting/success state stays
in flow. On mobile it clears the bottom navigation, and on desktop it sits 16px
above the viewport bottom. Saving, unsaved, error and success feedback share the
same region.

Dirty state compares the persisted profile fields and selected race target;
display-only time/pace equivalents do not count as edits. Successful saves update
the saved baseline even when the following read returns an unchanged snapshot.

Unsaved edits live in shell memory, including time/pace drafts, so Back/Forward
between portal destinations can restore them. Explicit navigation away and
logout offer discard confirmation; document unload uses the browser warning.
There is no draft storage persistence. This behavior documents the shipped flow,
not a new backend contract or a promise of recovery after reload.

### Onboarding

A compact labelled path leads one task heading and shared Connect/Profile
behavior. Native provider radios expose selection and capability differences;
there is no preselected provider. One button names the selected source and owns
authorization. A disclosure explains shared data. Status-query failures, pending
authorization, reconnect and connection errors retain actionable feedback.
Settings keep their existing provider composition and replacement dialogs.

The first-run profile is unboxed. Field labels replace redundant introductory
copy; weekly rhythm and conditional race details retain short headings.
Validation, the four-day minimum, long-run restrictions, target conversion and
save/draft protection remain. Future coach notes are omitted only in onboarding.

Preparation has one running mark, title and supporting sentence. Saved source
and profile facts sit behind a native disclosure. Missing, preparing, failed and
ready remain distinct bootstrap states, without generation percentages or
simulated milestones. Failure offers retry and native technical error details;
refresh/retry failures remain inline. Automatic readiness clears a stale retry
error. Returning users whose initial bootstrap is ready enter directly; an
ongoing journey waits for the explicit Open weekly plan action.

Provider selection and the current-step underline use shared-element springs;
heading layout preserves continuity and incoming tasks settle horizontally.
Confirmed connection and selected weekdays draw checks. The running mark gives
way to a check only on actual readiness. Backend step changes immediately remove
previous controls; exit animations cannot gate progression. Task changes focus
the heading; explicit portal entry focuses the mounted main workspace. Reduced
motion uses final states without spatial motion or drawing. The existing shell,
fonts, colors and controls connect setup to Weekly Plan. Onboarding's active
mobile save bar clears the safe area without reserving a destination bar.

### Weekly Plan

Screen-specific composition in `src/components/portal/WeeklyPlanScreen.tsx`:
week navigation, metrics, goal state and sessions. Today distinguishes today's
session and completion. Matched activities show provenance and Strava or

visible when manual completion changes; evidence and the checkbox are independent
states. Do not extract modality colors, intensities or the entire composition
into shared components without actual repetition.

## Do's and Don'ts

- **Do** reuse existing CSS roles and variants before adding values.
- **Do** preserve observed assets, typography and content when refining.
- **Do** follow AGENTS.md and define a mobile-first hierarchy before implementing
  new screens or structural changes; compare with the agreed design or brief.
- **Do** keep public composition and action overrides scoped to Landing and Login;
  preserve shared body type, theme switching and the separate privacy/portal systems.
- **Do** retain visible final content under reduced motion and distinguish an
  illustrative week from an athlete's actual plan.
- **Don't** treat this capture as an approved rebrand, a UX audit or accessibility
  certification for every theme.
- **Don't** create components from templates the product does not yet use.
- **Don't** invent shadows, percentage rules, a visual concept or a preferred
  final theme that the sources do not establish.
